## Business Problem

The company wants to understand how product pricing, customer discounts, sales volume, and manufacturing costs are affecting revenue and gross profitability. The analysis will identify products, customer segements, and sales channels that generate strong revenue but underperform in gross margin. 

## Objectives:
1. Measure revenue and gross profit.
2. Calculate gross margin.
3. Analyze pricing performance by product.
4. Identify low-margin/high-revenue products.
5. Analyze discount behavior.
6. COmpare customer segments.
7. Identify regional and sales-channel differences.
8. Identity potential pricing opportunities.
